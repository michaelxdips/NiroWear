WHY THIS IS COMPATIBLE WITH NIROWEAR'S MIT LICENSE
--------------------------------------------------
The library is GPL, not MIT. NiroWear does not relicense it and does not claim it as
first-party code. The library's own linking exception grants:

  "As a special exception, the copyright holders of this library give you permission to
   link this library with independent modules to produce an executable, regardless of the
   license terms of these independent modules, and to copy and distribute the resulting
   executable under terms of your choice, provided that you also meet, for each linked
   independent module, the terms and conditions of the license of that module. An
   independent module is a module that is not derived from or based on this library."

NiroWear's own source is such an independent module. The exception therefore permits this
combination and permits distributing the resulting APK under NiroWear's terms, while the
GPL continues to govern the JLaTeXMath code itself.

The obligation that remains, and which this file and the accompanying notices discharge, is
to pass on the library's license text and to point to its corresponding source.

BUNDLED FONTS
-------------
The library ships font resources with their own terms, reproduced in this directory:
OFL.txt (SIL Open Font License), Knuth_License.txt, License_for_dsrom.txt, and public-domain
fonts. The GPL-covered Greek font set listed in the upstream LICENSE (fcmbipg, fcmripg,
fcmrpg, fcsropg, fcmbpg, fcsbpg, fctrpg, fcsrpg) is NOT present in the 0.2.0 artifact and is
therefore not distributed with NiroWear.

The upstream LICENSE file also notes that the fonts eufb10.ttf, eufm10.ttf, msam10.ttf and
msbm10.ttf are under the SIL Open Font License, and that stmary10.ttf and rsfs10.ttf are in the
public domain. Those are the sets actually bundled here.
